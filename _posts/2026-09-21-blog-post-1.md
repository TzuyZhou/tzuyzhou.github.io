For a very long time I have been thinking about setting up a personal website, and it has finally come to life!

This site will primarily serve as a space for my academic work: a place to share my research, record ideas and thoughts, and document the things I find interesting along the way. I also hope to use it as a more personal space—a place to write about the things I enjoy outside of research, such as photography and other interests.

Think of it as a small corner of the internet where I can share what I am working on, what I am thinking about, and perhaps a few things I simply find beautiful or interesting.
