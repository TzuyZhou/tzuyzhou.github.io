When studying topics on integrable systems, it must be easy to notice a group of people that have promiently contributed to various aspects of this field in theoretical physics. Led by Ludwig Faddeev, this group of people standing at the frontier of the quantum integrable models are well-known as *the Leningrad School*. This blog will stand at a rather historical point of view to review the contributions by the Leningrad School, while necessary mathematical discussion will be presented as well.
